Delete your Lazy Vocabulary account

App: Lazy Vocabulary (com.hoctusach.lazyvocabulary) · Developer contact: hoctusach@gmail.com

Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open Lazy Vocabulary (Android app or website) and sign in.
  2. Go to the More tab → Privacy & account → Delete account.
  3. Type your nickname and enter your passcode to confirm, then tap Delete permanently.

The app deletes your data on our server and signs you out. Optionally tick Also remove imported word sets and local settings from this device to wipe local data too.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you forgot your passcode or cannot open the app, email hoctusach@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Lazy Vocabulary account and tell us your nickname. We may ask a question to confirm the account is yours (for example recent words you learned) because there is no email address on the profile. We aim to delete within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: nickname, passcode, profile key, time zone.
  • All synced progress: learned words and review schedule, progress summary, study dates and learning time.
  • Sign-in sessions and your friend-compare share link (the link stops working).

What is not deleted / retention

  • Imported word sets and settings live only on your device. They are not on our servers, so they are removed only if you tick the option above, delete the sets in the Sets screen, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Backups at our database provider (Supabase) may keep deleted rows for a short time before they expire; they are not used or restored except for disaster recovery.
  • Advertising data in the Android app is held by Google AdMob, not by us; manage it at Google My Ad Center. See the privacy policy.
